## Interpretation rules
1. Claim-level evidence strength differs from source-level classification. Do not silently upgrade a practitioner suggestion to an empirical finding.
2. Printed page ranges and headings above are navigation aids from uploaded files; verify exact passages and independent citations when operationalizing each criterion.
3. For modern web accessibility, consult current authoritative W3C normative text, current ACT rules and evaluation methodology. Determine local contractual/legal applicability separately.
4. For conversational or probabilistic AI interfaces, pursue contemporary research. S08 motivates questions, not modern AI safety claims.
5. Preserve citation and copyright boundaries: reference sources bibliographically, do not commit full copyrighted PDF text.
