## Scoring Criteria

Each project is scored on:

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implementation completeness | 40% | % of planned work items done |
| Spec/design completeness | 20% | Is the what-to-build clear? |
| Unblocked status | 20% | No hard external dependency blocking next step |
| Time-to-ship | 20% | How many work sessions to a shippable artifact |

Combined score yields a **deliverability %** and **rank**.
